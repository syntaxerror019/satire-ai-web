In a stunning display of linguistic audacity and geographical confusion, former President Donald Trump has reportedly suggested renaming the Pacific Ocean the “Ocean of Americas,” a move that experts say is “less about geography and more about making the ocean sound like a Trump rally.”  

“This isn’t just a name change—it’s a rebranding,” Trump allegedly declared at a rally in Clarksville, Tennessee, while gesturing wildly at a map that appeared to be a children’s coloring book. “The Pacific? Sounds too fancy. Too ‘elitist.’ The Ocean of Americas? That’s *classy*. That’s *power*. That’s *winning*.”  

Marine biologists, meanwhile, are in chaos. “We’re not sure if the fish will be confused or just relieved,” said Dr. Karen Wave, a marine biologist who has spent her career studying the Pacific’s ability to sustain life. “But if the ocean now has to answer to a name that sounds like a 2016 campaign slogan, I’m worried it might just give up and turn into a lake.”  

Trump’s plan, according to insiders, also includes renaming the Atlantic Ocean the “Atlantic of Trump” and the Gulf of Mexico the “Gulf of Greatness.” “We’re not just changing names—we’re changing *history*,” said a Trump spokesperson, who added that the administration is “exploring the possibility of renaming the moon ‘The Moon of Trump’ just to be thorough.”  

The move has already sparked international backlash, with the United Nations issuing a statement that reads, in full: “We are deeply concerned.” Meanwhile, the Oceanic Council of Nations has convened an emergency meeting to discuss whether the Pacific should legally change its name to “The Pacific of America” or simply “The Pacific of Trump.”  

As for the actual ocean? Sources say it’s “very conflicted.”
