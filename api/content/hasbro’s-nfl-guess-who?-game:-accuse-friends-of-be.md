Just in time for the 2026-27 NFL season, Hasbro has unleashed a “revolutionary” new way to ruin family game nights: *Guess Who?*—now with more frustration, fewer actual guesses, and a 48-person lineup of athletes who will *definitely* be Travis Kelce.  

The game, which somehow manages to be both a board game and a spiritual successor to the *Twilight Zone*, tasks players with asking yes/no questions to determine if their opponent is, in fact, the only person in the room who hasn’t yet learned the meaning of “personal boundaries.” The NFL edition features 24 AFC and 24 NFC players, though Hasbro assures us they’re “randomly divided” between the boards—because nothing says “sports entertainment” like a 50% chance of being stuck with a guy named “Jared Goff.”  

The most exciting feature? The ability to “close all your tabs once you guess correctly.” Because nothing says “family bonding” like a 12-year-old screaming, “IS HE FROM THE AFC?!” while their dad frantically Googles “how to win at life.”  

Amazon is selling the game for $15.26—$9.73 less than the price of your dignity. The box even includes a “fold-out” board that snaps together “for easy storage,” which is just code for “you’ll never find the pieces again.”  

Hasbro isn’t stopping there. They’ve also released a *Bop It!* game featuring Josh Allen, where players are challenged to “bop” the football star’s chest, twist his arm with the ball, or pull on his foot—all while a sports announcer-style voice yells, “HOLD ON, HOLD ON, HOLD ON!” into your ears. The figurines are “great for collectibles,” though we’re not sure if that’s a warning or a compliment.  

And if *Guess Who?* and *Bop It!* aren’t enough, Mattel has an *UNO* game with all 32 NFL logos, because nothing says “fun” like a card game where everyone’s constantly yelling, “PENALTY!” and “HAIL MARY!”  

The NFL season begins on Sept. 9, 2026, with the Seattle Seahawks—last year’s Super Bowl champions, who somehow won by defeating the New England Patriots in a game that was “more of a clinic than a contest”—ready to defend their title. Good luck, Seahawks. You’ll need it.
