In a stunning twist that no one saw coming, Fox & Friends has finally acknowledged the obvious: Mexico isn’t paying for that wall. Well, not in the way Trump envisioned, which was probably with a check written in glitter and signed by a llama.  

The show’s hosts, who have spent years treating the wall like a magical portal to Mexico’s treasury, finally admitted that their beloved president’s promise was as empty as a Trump Tower elevator during a blackout. “He’s right,” co-host Brian Kilmeade sighed, as if he’d just learned that the moon is made of cheese. “The president never should have said Mexico was gonna pay for the wall.”  

But don’t worry—there’s a plan! Instead of actually asking Mexico for money, the administration will now steal $3.6 billion from defense programs, including funds for a middle school, a fire station, and child development centers. Co-host Ainsley Earhardt explained this with the gravitas of a person who just discovered that their pet goldfish is actually a secret CIA agent. “We’re just… reallocating resources,” she said, as if reallocating resources was a normal thing to do when you’re literally building a wall out of spite.  

When pressed on the absurdity of this plan, Steve Doocy suggested that Congress could just hand the stolen money back to the same programs twice. “It’s not stealing if you do it twice,” he reasoned, as if he’d just invented the concept of “double taxation.”  

Meanwhile, Trump’s original promise—that Mexico would be “very happy” about paying for the wall—now seems as plausible as a unicorn winning a Nobel Prize. In 2016, he confidently declared the wall would be “complete within two years,” a timeline so optimistic it could have been written by a toddler on a sugar rush.  

As the wall remains a distant dream, Fox & Friends has finally accepted the truth: the only thing Mexico is paying for is the collective sanity of Americans who still believe in this.
